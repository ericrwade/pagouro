# The Pagouro mark

`pagouro_mark.png` (1254×1254) is the outward-facing mark: a hermit crab retreated into its shell,
claws folded and eyes forward, in a round teal medallion on cream, art-nouveau flourishes, a
scallop, and "Pagouro" on a lettered band — the Belle Époque house look (D-74).

**Provenance.** Made by Eric Wade, 2026-09-21, as one of ten concepts (`Pagouro_All_Concepts.zip`,
kept out of the repository; a contact sheet of all ten is `concepts/_contact_sheet.jpg`); he chose
#5. Generation tool: to be recorded here by Eric (the rights note on the release names it).
The mark is a brand asset, not training data: it is in no corpus ledger and no model was trained
on it. Rights: Eric's, released with the project under the same terms as the weights (CC BY-SA 4.0)
unless the release notes say otherwise; the name itself is subject to the trademark check (O-29).

**Files.** `pagouro_mark.png` full size; `pagouro_mark_{1024,512,256,128,64,32}.png` and
`pagouro_mark_512.jpg` derived by Lanczos; `pagouro.ico` (256→16 px) for the executable;
`mark_house_64.json` + `pagouro_mark_house_64.png` — the mark snapped to the 32-colour house
palette with dithering, what `/art logo` draws in the terminal (32 px was tried and the lettering
turned to mush, so 64 is the smallest shipped).
