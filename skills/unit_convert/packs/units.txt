Unit conversion reference (Pagouro unit_convert skill, CC0). Exact factors unless marked approximate.

Length. 1 inch = 25.4 millimetres exactly. 1 foot = 12 inches = 0.3048 metre. 1 yard = 3 feet = 0.9144 metre. 1 mile = 5,280 feet = 1,609.344 metres. 1 nautical mile = 1,852 metres. 1 kilometre = 0.621371 mile (approximate).

Mass. 1 pound = 16 ounces = 0.45359237 kilogram exactly. 1 ounce = 28.349523 grams. 1 stone = 14 pounds = 6.35029 kilograms. 1 kilogram = 2.20462 pounds (approximate). 1 tonne = 1,000 kilograms.

Volume (US customary). 1 US teaspoon = 4.92892 millilitres. 1 US tablespoon = 3 teaspoons = 14.7868 millilitres. 1 US fluid ounce = 29.5735 millilitres. 1 US cup = 8 fluid ounces = 236.588 millilitres. 1 US pint = 2 cups = 473.176 millilitres. 1 US quart = 2 pints = 0.946353 litre. 1 US gallon = 4 quarts = 3.785411784 litres exactly. A metric cup (used in recipes from Australia and elsewhere) is 250 millilitres; a UK imperial pint is 568 millilitres, not 473.

Temperature. Celsius to Fahrenheit: multiply by 9/5 and add 32. Fahrenheit to Celsius: subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9. Kelvin = Celsius + 273.15. Water freezes at 0 °C = 32 °F and boils at 100 °C = 212 °F at sea level.

Data. 1 kilobyte (kB) = 1,000 bytes; 1 kibibyte (KiB) = 1,024 bytes. 1 megabyte = 1,000,000 bytes; 1 mebibyte = 1,048,576 bytes. 1 gigabyte = 10^9 bytes; 1 gibibyte = 2^30 = 1,073,741,824 bytes. Operating systems differ in which they display.

Speed. 1 metre per second = 3.6 kilometres per hour = 2.23694 miles per hour. 1 knot = 1 nautical mile per hour = 1.852 km/h = 1.15078 mph.

Time. 1 minute = 60 seconds. 1 hour = 3,600 seconds. 1 day = 86,400 seconds. 1 week = 604,800 seconds.
